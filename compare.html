How AddTxt compares

AddTxt set out to do everything the best-known text-on-photo app does, without the adverts, and to add what it leaves out. This page sets the two side by side, including where the other app does more.

The right-hand column states only what Add Text: Text on Photo Editor's own Google Play listing said when it was read on 17 November 2026. "Not mentioned on its listing" means exactly that: the app may do it, but its listing does not say so.

Where the other app does more

  • It has more shapes and more stickers.
  • It lets you save your own text styles. AddTxt's styles are ready-made.
  • It saves WebP files. AddTxt saves PNG, JPG and PDF.
  • It is on Google Play, so it updates itself. AddTxt tells you when a new version is out and you install it from this site.

Where AddTxt does more

  • No adverts, and no paid version.
  • A pen and marker, and brushes that pixelate or blur part of a photo.
  • Safety signs and site symbols in the standard colours, and a key box that lists your shapes and lines by colour.
  • Brand kits, and the same layers on a whole batch of photos.
  • Text along a line you draw with your finger, lists, a second outline and a special-character picker.
