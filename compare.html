How AddTxt compares

AddTxt set out to do everything the best-known text-on-photo app does, without the adverts, and to add what it leaves out. This page sets the two side by side, including where the other app does more.

Where the other app does more

It has more shapes and more stickers, lets you save your own text styles, saves WebP files, and is on Google Play, so it updates itself.

Where AddTxt does more

No adverts and no paid version. A pen and brushes that pixelate or blur, safety signs and site symbols with a key box, brand kits, batches, and text along a line you draw.
