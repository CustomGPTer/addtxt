Licences

AddTxt is built with open-source software and open fonts. Thank you to the people who make them.

Fonts in the app

The 1,939 font families offered in AddTxt come from Google Fonts. Each is published under one of three open licences, all of which allow personal and commercial use, including in pictures you sell: the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (most of them), the Apache License 2.0, or the Ubuntu Font Licence 1.0. Each family's page on Google Fonts names its designer and its licence. The app's own headings are set in Space Grotesk by Florian Karsten, under the SIL Open Font License.

Signs and patterns

The safety signs and site symbols are drawn by the app from the standard colours and outlines, with pictograms taken from Google's Material icons. The pattern fills are worked out by the app and contain no artwork from anywhere else.

This website

The site is set in Literata and Atkinson Hyperlegible, both under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 and served from this site.
