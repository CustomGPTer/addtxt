Release notes

What changed in each version of AddTxt. The newest is at the top.

Version 1.0 17 November 2026Latest

  • Text on photos or on a blank canvas, with 1,939 fonts: 170 built in, the rest downloaded the first time each is used, plus your own font files.
  • 92 ready-made styles in nine groups, and styles made from the brand kit in use.
  • Styling for selected words: bold, italic, underline, strike through, colour and size. Bullet, number and tick lists. A special-character picker.
  • Colour tools: a full picker, a dropper that takes a colour from the picture, saved and recent colours, colours pulled from the photo, and gradients.
  • Outline and second outline, shadow, glow, marker band, box, 3D depth, tilt, 24 pattern fills and photo fill.
  • Text bent into an arc or a wave, or along a line drawn with a finger.
  • Photo layers, 24 shapes with words inside them, emoji, 42 safety signs, 30 site symbols and your own saved stickers.
  • Pen, marker, pixelate brush and blur brush, with a straight-line option.
  • 19 picture effects, adjust, crop, resize, turn and flip, on the background and on photo layers.
  • Rub out part of any layer, remove a colour, and an optional automatic cut-out that is off until switched on.
  • Layers: reorder, lock, hide, rename and group. A history list of every step.
  • Snap guides, a grid, and an optional key box.
  • Brand kits with colours, two fonts and a logo each.
  • Batch: the same layers on any number of other photos.
  • Saving as PNG, JPG or PDF to the AddTxt album, at full size or smaller, with the photo's date and location kept unless switched off.
  • Projects saved as you work, dark, light and match-the-phone themes, upright and sideways layouts.
  • A notice when a newer version is out, which can be switched off.

Checking your download

The file for version 1.0 is called AddTxt.apk. It is 22.4 MB (22,392,707 bytes). Its SHA-256 checksum is:

4c8130f241fd9da0908e8e3c9214b282cf75142b6c2dd8f74b6be84d6ed5661e

On Windows, run certutil -hashfile AddTxt.apk SHA256 and compare. Every version is signed with the same key, so each one installs over the last and keeps what you have saved.

Older versions are on the GitHub releases page.
