Put words on pictures, properly

AddTxt is an Android app for adding text to photos. It has the fonts, styles, layers, shapes and stickers you would expect, and the things that are usually missing: styling for single words, text along any line you draw, a pen, brand kits, and the same title block on a whole batch of photos.

It is free, with no adverts and nothing to unlock. Everything is done on your phone, and every word stays editable after you save.

How it works

A photo with a clear label on it gets read. AddTxt is built so that making one takes a minute, and so that nothing you add is ever stuck where you first put it.

  1. Pick a photo

    Open one from your gallery, send one to AddTxt with Share, or start from a blank canvas in a colour or gradient.

  2. Add your words

    Tap Add text, type, and drag it into place. Pick a ready-made style or set the font, colour and effects yourself.

  3. Save or share

    Tap Save for a PNG, JPG or PDF in your gallery. The project is kept, so you can come back and change any word later.

Everything is in, and all of it is free

A caption takes two taps. The rest is there for the days you need more: a marked-up site photo, a poster, a logo on fifty pictures, a face that should not be shown.

1,939 fonts
170 are built in and work with no signal. The rest come from Google Fonts the first time you pick one, then stay on the phone. You can add your own font files too.
92 ready-made styles
Bold, outline, 3D, neon, retro, script, label, site and pattern looks. One tap sets the font, colours and effects; your words, size and position stay as they are.
Style single words
Select words while you type and make just those bold, italic, underlined, struck through, a different colour, bigger or smaller. Bullet, number and tick lists too.
Text on any line
Bend text into an arc or a wave, or draw a line across the picture with your finger and the words run along it.
Outlines, shadows, glow, 3D
Two outlines in different colours, soft or hard shadows, glow, solid 3D depth, and tilt that makes text sit on a wall or the ground.
Patterns and photo fill
24 built-in patterns, from hazard stripes to gold, or fill the letters with one of your own photos. Gradients as well.
Photos, shapes and stickers
Add photos as layers, 24 shapes with words inside them, emoji, 42 safety signs and 30 site symbols in the standard colours, and your own saved stickers.
Pen, marker, pixelate and blur
Draw freehand or in straight lines. Paint over a face or a number plate to pixelate or blur it.
Picture tools
19 effects, brightness, contrast, colour, warmth and blur, crop, resize, turn and flip, on the background or on any photo layer.
Cut out and rub out
Remove a plain colour, such as the white behind a logo, with one touch. Rub out part of any layer. An optional automatic cut-out lifts a person or object so text can sit behind them.
Layers and history
Reorder, lock, hide, rename and group layers. Every step is listed, so you can go back to any point. Nothing is baked in: every word stays editable.
Guides and grid
Text clicks into line with the centre, the edges and other layers as you drag it, with a grid when you want one.
Brand kits
Keep each company's or project's colours, fonts and logo together. The kit in use leads every colour row and sets the font for new text.
Batch
Put the same text, logo or title block on any number of other photos in one go. Each is saved with the layers in the same place, sized to fit.
A key box
An optional legend that lists every shape and pen line by colour, using the names you gave the layers.
PNG, JPG and PDF
Save at full size or smaller, with a see-through background where you want one. The photo's date and location stay on the saved picture unless you switch that off.

What it looks like

Dark by default, with a light theme and one that follows the phone. It works upright and on its side.

What it asks of your phone

AddTxt has no access to your contacts, camera, microphone or location. It does not ask for access to your gallery either: you hand it each photo yourself with the phone's own picker.

Questions

Is AddTxt really free?

Yes. There is no paid version, no subscription and no adverts, and nothing in the app is locked.

Is AddTxt on Google Play?

No. AddTxt is a personal project and is downloaded from this site as an Android app file. It needs Android 11 or later.

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. AddTxt reads and saves pictures on your phone, and it has no servers. It goes online for two things only: to fetch a font you pick, and to look for a newer version of itself.

Does it work with no signal?

Yes. The 170 built-in fonts and every tool work offline. Only fonts you have not used before, and the first set-up of the optional automatic cut-out, need the internet.

What is the automatic cut-out, and why is it off?

It finds the person or object in a photo by itself. It is made by Google, runs on the phone and never uploads a picture, but Google Play has to download it once and it reports details of the phone and how well it ran to Google. So it stays off, and nothing of it runs, until you switch it on in Settings.

Can I change the text after saving?

Yes. Saving makes a picture in your gallery, and the project stays in AddTxt with every layer still editable.

Are the safety signs official?

No. They use the standard colours and outlines with general pictograms, which is right for a briefing slide, a plan or a marked-up photo. They are not the official artwork and are no substitute for the printed signs a site needs.

Does the saved picture keep the photo's date and place?

By default, yes, so it sorts beside the original. One switch in Settings removes them from everything you save, which is worth doing before sharing outside your team.

Where are my pictures saved?

Pictures go to the AddTxt album in Pictures, and PDFs to Documents, AddTxt. Projects are kept inside the app and are not backed up anywhere.

How do I update AddTxt?

AddTxt tells you on its home screen when a newer version is out. Download it from this site and install it over the one you have. Your projects and settings are kept.
